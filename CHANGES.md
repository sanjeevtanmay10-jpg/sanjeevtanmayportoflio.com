# Version 2 — what changed

The live site (https://sanjeevtanmay.pages.dev) and the `main` branch are untouched. This folder is the new version.

## Case studies
- SplitRight, Atelier and Forward 2027 are now written case studies: real text in chapters (problem, solution, research, flows, design system, wireframes, final screens), with images between them.
- Every image opens full size when clicked (scroll to read the small details).
- SplitRight and Atelier images were re-exported from the original files at 2400px wide, so they stay sharp on retina and phone screens.
- Forward 2027 and Postfolio still use the 1280px images: no higher-resolution files were provided for them.

## Home page
- Start screen shows your photo, name and "UX/UI designer portfolio".
- Hero has a role line and a "See the projects" button.
- Projects now come right after the hero; About me follows.
- The big live-stats box is gone; the counts are a small line under Reviews ("34 visits, 4 likes, 1 review").
- The header gets a solid, blurred background once you scroll, so text no longer runs under it.
- Project cards show type and role, use new covers (SplitRight without the cut-off "Overview", Postfolio showing actual posters) and share one card style with the review boxes.
- Sanju's empty seats show a faint outline, so his lines never float on their own.
- The intro starts speaking after about 1 second instead of 2.5.

## Quality
- Colour contrast checked with axe on every page at desktop and phone sizes: no failures.
- The menu button now has a name for screen readers on phones.
- Fonts are self-hosted (faster, no Google Fonts request).
